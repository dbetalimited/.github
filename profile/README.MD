## Overview

DBETA Bones is a software platform for organisations that need more than a collection of disconnected web pages.

It is designed around structured business information. Organisations can define the types of information they manage—such as services, projects, cases, people, locations or assets—and connect them through meaningful relationships.

The same governed information can then support administration, publishing, websites, integrations and machine-readable outputs.

## Who Bones Is For

Bones is intended for organisations whose websites and digital services depend on structured, interconnected information.

Typical users may include:

- content and operations teams
- digital and product teams
- developers and implementation partners
- governance and quality teams
- organisations preparing for AI-driven discovery

Bones is particularly relevant where information must remain consistent, reusable and manageable across multiple digital outputs.

## Problems Bones Solves

Digital platforms often grow into collections of pages, databases, integrations and structured-data scripts that must be maintained separately.

This can create:

- rigid content and data structures
- duplicated or conflicting information
- expensive custom development
- disconnected websites and integrations
- weak publishing control
- poor traceability
- outdated machine-readable information
- dependence on templates or page-builder structures
- difficulty reusing or exporting information

Bones addresses these problems by keeping structured business information at the centre of the platform.

Instead of maintaining separate versions of the same facts for different channels, organisations can manage governed information once and use it across appropriate digital outputs.

## How Bones Differs

Bones is not primarily a page builder or a conventional content-management system.

| Approach | Primary focus |
|---|---|
| Traditional CMS | Pages and predefined content types |
| Page builder | Visual page composition |
| Headless CMS | Structured content delivered through APIs |
| Business application | A fixed operational model and workflow |
| **Bones** | User-defined business information connected to publishing and digital delivery |

Bones combines the flexibility of a configurable information system with the publishing and presentation capabilities of a digital platform.

## How It Works

```text
Define business information
            ↓
Connect fields and relationships
            ↓
Create and manage records
            ↓
Review and publish
            ↓
Use the information across digital outputs
```

The intention is simple: information should be structured and governed before it is turned into pages, integrations or machine-readable output.

## Core Product Areas

### Structured Information

Organisations can define and connect the information that matters to them instead of being limited to a fixed set of generic content types.

### Administration

Bones is intended to give teams a consistent place to manage records, relationships and publishing activity.

### Publishing and Presentation

Governed information can support websites and other digital experiences while remaining separate from any single design or template.

### Machine Legibility

Bones includes AIDI—AI Discovery Infrastructure—to help make appropriate published information understandable to search engines, AI systems and other machine consumers.

AIDI output is derived from governed organisational information rather than maintained as an unrelated copy.

### Extensions and Integrations

Bones is intended to support additional capabilities and connections without losing control of the underlying information.

## The Bones Ecosystem

Bones is intended to grow beyond the core platform into a broader ecosystem.

Over time, this may include:

- industry solution modules
- themes and presentation packages
- approved integrations
- AI assistants and task-specific bots
- browser tools and extensions
- AIDI validation and discovery tools
- developer and partner capabilities
- a governed marketplace

For example, future AI assistants could support enquiries, sales, bookings, customer service, internal knowledge or content workflows while working with structured organisational information.

Browser tools could help people inspect machine-readable information, identify inconsistencies or connect approved research and operational activity to Bones.

Marketplace and third-party distribution capabilities would be introduced only when the appropriate review, compatibility and lifecycle controls are in place.

This is a statement of product direction, not a release schedule or a claim that every capability is currently available.

## Current Status

Bones 9 is under active development and is not currently offered as a self-service public download.

The platform is being developed and validated progressively. Capabilities described in this README may represent a mixture of current development, planned product functionality and longer-term direction.

Organisations interested in future availability, pilots or implementation partnerships can contact DBETA.

## Principles

Bones is guided by several core principles:

- business information comes before pages
- organisations should be able to define their own information structures
- one governed source should support multiple appropriate outputs
- publishing should be deliberate
- machine-readable information should remain connected to its source
- presentation should not own business truth
- important activity should be manageable and verifiable
- organisations should retain control of their information
